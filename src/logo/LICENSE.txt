linlog's logo

The files of this directory are linlog's logo: the word mark
(wordmark-light.svg, wordmark-dark.svg, wordmark-mono.svg), its square form
(square-light.svg, square-dark.svg, square-mono.svg), and the images drawn
from them (the favicon, the app icons, the Apple touch icon, the link
preview's image).

The name "linlog" and its mark identify the linlog project
(https://github.com/linlog-prover/linlog). They may not be used in a way
that suggests that the project or its author endorses anything else.

The word is set in Figtree Bold and outlined, so no font file goes with
these files. Figtree is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose
condition 5 exempts a document created using the font from that licence.
