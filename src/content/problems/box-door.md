title: "A door that is not under ?"
sequent: "!(A -o B), A |- !B"

`!B` needs a box, and a box's other doors must all be under `?`. One-sided they are `?(A ⊗ B⊥)` and `A⊥`, and `A⊥` is not: whatever is copied from `!(A ⊸ B)`, the one `A` cannot go into the box.
