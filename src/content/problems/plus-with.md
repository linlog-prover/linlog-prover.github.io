---
title: "Plus does not give with"
sequent: "A + B |- A & B"
mode: "classical"
verdict: "unprovable"
level: 2
categories: [additives, classical]
fragment: "ALL"
lesson: 3
order: 14
---
`&` asks for both `A` and `B` from the same context, and `A ⊕ B` gives one of them. Read classically the sequent is false when `A` is true and `B` false.
