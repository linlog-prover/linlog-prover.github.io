The image of Peirce's law, `((A → B) → A) → A`, under the translation of classical logic into affine MALL, as `linlog seq print --logic classical` prints it. `&` gives two goals; in the first, `B` is left over and weakening drops it. Without weakening, in classical mode, the image has no proof.
