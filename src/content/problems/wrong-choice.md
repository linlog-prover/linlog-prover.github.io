Take `B` from `A & B`, the `B` that `B ⊕ D` needs: one-sided, `⊕₂` chooses `B⊥` from `A⊥ ⊕ B⊥`. Choosing `A⊥` loses the proof at once: `A⊥` then has no `A` to meet.
