One-sided, the formula is `((((⊤ ⅋ 1) ⊗ ⊥) ⅋ 0) ⊗ ⊤) ⅋ 1`, and its proof keeps `1` beside `⊤` and `0` in one goal: `⊤` closes it. Intuitionistic mode has no goal with two formulas on the right, so the same text has no proof there.
