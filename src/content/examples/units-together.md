---
title: One, top and zero together
sequent: "(A * 1) & (1 * A) |- A * (1 + 0)"
mode: classical
difficulty: 3
verdict: provable
lesson: 4
order: 143
---
Either side of the hypothesis gives `A` and a `1`, which is a `⊥` on the right and goes away; the split sends `A⊥` to `A` and nothing to `1 ⊕ 0`, where `⊕` takes `1`, since `0` has no rule.
