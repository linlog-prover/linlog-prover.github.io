title: About
---
This page is [linlog](https://github.com/linlog-prover/linlog)'s prover for
linear logic, running in your browser: you type a sequent and prove it step by
step, or let linlog's search close a goal. linlog's library is compiled to
WebAssembly and runs in the page; nothing you type is sent anywhere, and your
proofs stay in this browser's storage.

The library's documentation is at [/linlog/](/linlog/), its source and the
command line prover on [GitHub](https://github.com/linlog-prover/linlog).
Questions, mistakes and wishes are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/linlog-prover/linlog-prover.github.io/issues).
