---
title: Tutorial
---
Lessons that perform real steps on the stage and teach the logic, from reading a
sequent to proof nets. Each of the first five plays a worked proof one inference at a
time, asking you to predict each rule before it shows the step and its reason; then it
leaves the same proof's last steps to you, gives practice, and ends with a check. The
later lessons have their practice so far, sequents with a proof and without, each with
its answer and, where there is a proof, a proof to watch. The tour shows the page itself.
